The enchanter is a master of enchanting armor and tools. Use the Enchanter's power-ups to get more experience for enchanting!

## How to Get Experience

-   Enchanting items and books

## Power-ups

| Power-Up             | Description                                            | Required Level     | Cost         |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------ | ------------ |
| Job Experience I     | Multiply your job experience gain by 1.5x.             | Enchanter level 15 | 5 job coins  |
| Job Experience II    | Multiply your job experience gain by 2x.               | Enchanter level 25 | 8 job coins  |
| Job Experience III   | Multiply your job experience gain by 3x.               | Enchanter level 40 | 12 job coins |
| Experience Boost I   | Multiply your experience from blocks and mobs by 1.5x. | Enchanter level 15 | 5 job coins  |
| Experience Boost II  | Multiply your experience from blocks and mobs by 2x.   | Enchanter level 30 | 8 job coins  |
| Experience Boost III | Multiply your experience from blocks and mobs by 2.5x. | Enchanter level 50 | 10 job coins |
