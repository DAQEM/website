The smith is a master of smelting ores and crafting tools. Use the Smith's power-ups to make your armor last longer!

## How to Get Experience

-   Using a grindstone
-   Smelting ores
-   Crafting tools
-   Crafting armor
-   Using an anvil

## Power-ups

| Power-Up             | Description                                            | Required Level | Cost         |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ | -------------- | ------------ |
| Armor Unbreaking I   | Armor is 10% less likely to take damage when attacked. | Smith level 5  | 5 job coins  |
| Armor Unbreaking II  | Armor is 20% less likely to take damage when attacked. | Smith level 15 | 8 job coins  |
| Armor Unbreaking III | Armor is 30% less likely to take damage when attacked. | Smith level 30 | 10 job coins |
| Armor Unbreaking IV  | Armor is 40% less likely to take damage when attacked. | Smith level 50 | 12 job coins |
| Armor Unbreaking V   | Armor is 50% less likely to take damage when attacked. | Smith level 80 | 15 job coins |
| Job Experience I     | Multiply your job experience gain by 1.5x.             | Smith level 15 | 5 job coins  |
| Job Experience II    | Multiply your job experience gain by 2x.               | Smith level 25 | 8 job coins  |
| Job Experience III   | Multiply your job experience gain by 3x.               | Smith level 40 | 12 job coins |
