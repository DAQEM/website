The alchemist is a master of potions and brewing. Use the Alchemist's power-ups to make your potions even stronger!

## How to Get Experience

-   Killing alchemy mobs (phantoms, pufferfish, magma cubes, ghasts, rabbits, spiders, cave spiders, blazes, and witches)
-   Brewing potions
-   Crafting potion ingredients (brewing stands, glistering melon slices, sugar, blaze powder, fermented spider eyes, magma cream, golden carrots, and turtle shells)
-   Throwing potions
-   Drinking potions
-   Harvesting Nether Wart
