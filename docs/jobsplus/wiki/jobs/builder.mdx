The builder is a master of building. Use the Builder's power-ups to take less fall damage, which is useful for building tall structures!

## How to Get Experience

-   Placing blocks

## Power-ups

| Power-Up             | Description                | Required Level   | Cost         |
| -------------------- | -------------------------- | ---------------- | ------------ |
| Less Fall Damage I   | Take 20% less fall damage. | Builder level 15 | 5 job coins  |
| Less Fall Damage II  | Take 40% less fall damage. | Builder level 30 | 8 job coins  |
| Less Fall Damage III | Take 60% less fall damage. | Builder level 50 | 10 job coins |
