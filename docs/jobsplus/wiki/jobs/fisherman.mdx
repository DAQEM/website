The fisherman is a master of fishing. Use the Fisherman's power-ups to use your fishing rod as a grappling hook!

## How to Get Experience

-   Catching fish and other items
-   Swimming

## Power-ups

| Power-Up           | Description                                                                              | Required Level     | Cost         |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------ | ------------ |
| Grappling Hook I   | Turns your fishing rod into a grappling hook that can be used to pull you to a location. | Fisherman level 10 | 5 job coins  |
| Grappling Hook II  | Make your grappling hook pull you further.                                               | Fisherman level 25 | 8 job coins  |
| Grappling Hook III | Make your grappling hook pull you even further.                                          | Fisherman level 40 | 10 job coins |
| Job Experience I   | Multiply your job experience gain by 1.5x.                                               | Fisherman level 15 | 5 job coins  |
| Job Experience II  | Multiply your job experience gain by 2x.                                                 | Fisherman level 25 | 8 job coins  |
| Job Experience III | Multiply your job experience gain by 3x.                                                 | Fisherman level 40 | 12 job coins |
