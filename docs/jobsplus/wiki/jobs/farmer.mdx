The farmer is a master of farming crops and animals. Use the Farmer's power-ups to get more drops from crops!

## How to Get Experience

-   Planting crops
-   Harvesting crops
-   Taming animals
-   Breeding animals

## Power-ups

| Power-Up           | Description                                                    | Required Level  | Cost         |
| ------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------- | ------------ |
| Double Drops I     | Grants a 5% chance to get double drops when harvesting crops.  | Farmer level 10 | 5 job coins  |
| Double Drops II    | Grants a 10% chance to get double drops when harvesting crops. | Farmer level 25 | 10 job coins |
| Double Drops III   | Grants a 20% chance to get double drops when harvesting crops. | Farmer level 50 | 15 job coins |
| Job Experience I   | Multiply your job experience gain by 1.5x.                     | Farmer level 15 | 5 job coins  |
| Job Experience II  | Multiply your job experience gain by 2x.                       | Farmer level 25 | 8 job coins  |
| Job Experience III | Multiply your job experience gain by 3x.                       | Farmer level 40 | 12 job coins |
