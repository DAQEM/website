If you need help with any of our mods, have found a bug, or just want to ask a question, you've come to the right place. To ensure you get the help you need as quickly as possible, please use the appropriate channel for your inquiry.

*   **[Reporting Issues](./reporting-issues.mdx)**
*   For bugs, crashes, or unexpected behavior. This is the best way to ensure that problems with the mod get fixed. Please use our GitHub issue tracker for this.

*   **[Discord](./discord.mdx)**
*   For general questions, help with configuration, suggestions, and community discussion. This is the place for more informal conversation.

### Supporting the Project

Our mods are free and open-source projects. If you find them useful and wish to support their continued development, you can do so through the author's Ko-fi page.

-   **[❤️ Support DAQEM on Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/daqem)**
