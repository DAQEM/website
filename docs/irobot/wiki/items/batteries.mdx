Batteries are the power source for your robots. A robot cannot function without one installed. There are three tiers of batteries, each offering a higher energy capacity.

## Tiers and Crafting

### Small Battery
The most basic battery, required for crafting robots.
*   **Capacity:** 500 (configurable)
