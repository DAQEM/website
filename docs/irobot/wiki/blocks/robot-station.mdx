### Functionality
* **Automatic Charging:** When a robot's energy level drops below a configurable threshold, it will automatically search for the nearest Robot Station and move on top of it to recharge.
* **Item Charging:** You can also charge Battery items by dropping them as an item entity on top of the Robot Station.
* **Charge Rate:** The rate of charging can be adjusted in the irobot-common.yml configuration file (robot_station.charge_rate_per_tick).
