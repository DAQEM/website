The **Gyro Robot** is the advanced tier of robot. It glides smoothly on a single wheel, using gyroscopic stabilization. While functionally similar to the Mini Robot, it is built from a more advanced chassis.

## Crafting

To craft a Gyro Robot, you will need a **Rolling Robot Chassis**, a **Dispenser**, a **Small Battery**, and two **Redstone Blocks**.

## Usage
Like the Mini Robot, place the Gyro Robot item on the ground to spawn it. The player who places it becomes its owner. You can access its interface by right-clicking it to manage its inventory, equipment, and task.
