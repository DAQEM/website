The **Mini Robot** is the first tier of robot you can craft. It's a versatile, bipedal machine that can perform all the essential tasks, serving as a great introduction to the mod's automation systems.

## Crafting

To craft a Mini Robot, you will need a **Basic Robot Chassis**, a **Dispenser**, a **Small Battery**, and two **Redstone Blocks**.

## Usage
Once crafted, place the Mini Robot item on the ground to spawn it. The player who places the robot will become its owner. Right-click the robot to open its interface, where you can manage its inventory, battery, modules, and armor.
