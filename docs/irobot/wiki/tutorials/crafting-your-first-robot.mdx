Welcome to the world of robotics! This tutorial will guide you through the process of crafting and activating your first **Mini Robot**.

## Step 1: Gather Basic Resources
Before you can build complex machines, you'll need some fundamental materials:
-   **Iron Ingots:** You'll need a lot of these.
-   **Redstone Dust:** For all the electronic components.
-   **Gold Ingots** and **Quartz:** For the microchip.
-   **Coal:** To craft a Coal Block for the battery.
-   **A Dispenser** and **a Chest**.

## Step 2: Craft the Core Components
With your resources gathered, it's time to craft the essential parts.

## Step 4: Activate Your Robot!
You now have a [Mini Robot](../robots/mini-robot.mdx) item in your inventory. To bring it to life, simply place it on the ground like you would a block.
<Admonition type="success" title="Congratulations!">
    You've just spawned your first robot! The player who places the robot automatically becomes its owner. Right-click your new companion to open its interface and see its inventory and stats. It is now ready to be assigned a task.
</Admonition>

## Next Steps: Power Up!
Your new robot is ready, but it doesn't have a battery yet. The next crucial step is to build a robot station to charge your robot's battery.
