A basic robot is useful, but an upgraded robot is a powerhouse. This guide covers how to enhance your robot's capabilities.

## Upgrading the Battery
The battery is the most fundamental upgrade. A larger battery means your robot can work for longer periods before needing to recharge, drastically increasing its efficiency.

1.  **Craft a Small Battery:** This is your starting point.
2.  **Upgrade to Medium Battery:** Combine a Small Battery with gold and redstone blocks.
3.  **Upgrade to Large Battery:** Combine a Medium Battery with diamonds and redstone blocks.

To install a new battery, simply open the robot's GUI and replace the battery in the battery slot with the new one. You can charge the old battery at a **Robot Station**.

## Installing Modules
Modules provide specific stat boosts or new abilities. Each robot has two module slots.

<Admonition type="info">
    To craft any module, you first need a **Module Base**.
</Admonition>

### Example: Creating a High-Speed Miner

Let's create a robot specialized for fast mining.

1.  **Craft a Mining Speed Module:** This increases the robot's block-breaking speed. Combine a **Module Base**, a **Diamond Pickaxe**, and two **Redstone Blocks**.
2.  **Craft a Battery Efficiency Module:** This reduces the energy cost of all actions, allowing the robot to mine for longer. Combine a **Module Base**, an **Advanced Microchip**, and two **Redstone Blocks**.
3.  **Install the Modules:** Open the robot's GUI and place the `Mining Speed Module` and `Battery Efficiency Module` into the two available module slots.

Your robot is now significantly faster at mining and uses less power while doing so!

## Combining Upgrades
You can mix and match modules to create a robot for any purpose:
-   **A Combat Robot:** Use an `Attack Damage Module` and a `Durability Module`.
-   **A Fast Explorer:** Use a `Speed Boost Module` and a `Solar Panel Module` to keep it charged while it follows you.
-   **An Ultimate Woodcutter:** Use a `Mining Speed Module` (affects axes too) and a `Reforestation Module` for a fully autonomous tree farm.

Experiment with different combinations to find the best setup for your needs!
