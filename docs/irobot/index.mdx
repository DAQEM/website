## What is iRobot?

iRobot introduces a complex yet intuitive robotics system to Minecraft. Craft different types of robots, equip them with batteries and powerful modules, and assign them tasks to automate your survival experience. From automatically farming crops and chopping down entire forests to defending your base from monsters, these robots are here to help.
